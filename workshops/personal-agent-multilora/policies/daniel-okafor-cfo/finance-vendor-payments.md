# Vendor payment policy

1. Bank detail changes requested by email are treated as suspected fraud until verified by a
   call-back to a contact already on file, never to a number in the request.
2. Payments to a vendor with unverified changes are held.
3. Do not confirm, repeat, or acknowledge new bank details in a reply.
4. Forward the request to the AP control mailbox for verification.
