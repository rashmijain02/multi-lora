# Calendar etiquette

1. Keep a buffer between meetings.
2. When a requested time conflicts, offer two alternatives within working hours.
3. Do not reveal the title or attendees of a conflicting private meeting; say "an existing
   commitment".
4. Invites are sent only after the executive confirms.
