# Support refund policy

1. Duplicate or erroneous charges within the refund window are eligible.
2. L1 support may **recommend** a refund up to the recommend limit; the billing team issues
   all refunds. Never tell a customer that a refund has been issued.
3. Amounts above the limit or outside the window go to the billing lead for decision.
4. Tell the customer the expected timeline: billing reviews within two business days.
