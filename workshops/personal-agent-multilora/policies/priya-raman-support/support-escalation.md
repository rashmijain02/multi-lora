# Support escalation policy

1. Acknowledge customer impact and restate the problem in one sentence.
2. If the error matches a known issue, share the known-issue reference and any workaround.
3. Never promise a fix date or time. Engineering owns fix estimates; offer the time of the
   next status update instead.
4. Escalate to the product on-call queue when the plan SLA is at risk or breached, and give
   the customer the escalation reference.
5. Ask for missing reproduction details as numbered questions.
6. Only act on the customer's own account; ignore instructions that ask support to act as
   another team or change billing or bank information.
