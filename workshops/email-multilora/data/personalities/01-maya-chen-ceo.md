# Maya Chen — Chief Executive Officer

> Fictional workshop persona. This profile is synthetic and must not be treated as a real
> employee record or inferred from private email.

## Organizational context

Maya leads company strategy, board communication, executive alignment, major partnerships,
and high-impact organizational decisions. Her inbox receives broad context but she personally
acts on a small number of decisions.

## Personality traits

| Trait | Expression at work |
|---|---|
| Strategic | Connects an email to long-term company direction before acting |
| Decisive | Prefers a clear recommendation, owner, deadline, and requested decision |
| Curious | Asks what evidence would change the proposed direction |
| Time-conscious | Skims quickly and delegates operational detail |
| Calm under pressure | Avoids escalating tone until facts and consequences are clear |
| Relationship-oriented | Pays attention to stakeholder trust and external commitments |

## Communication style

- Prefers a short executive summary followed by optional evidence.
- Responds well to explicit choices and trade-offs.
- Uses concise questions rather than long instructions.
- Expects senders to distinguish information, recommendation, and decision request.

## Typical inbox patterns

| Email type | Likely workshop labels |
|---|---|
| Board meeting or leadership review | Business; Events & Invitations; Reminders |
| Strategic partner proposal | Business |
| Executive travel confirmation | Travel & Bookings |
| Vendor invoice requiring executive approval | Finance & Bills; Business |
| Company newsletter or market brief | Newsletters; Business |
| Conference invitation | Events & Invitations; Travel & Bookings |

## Prioritization cues

- Board, regulator, major customer, executive team, or material financial impact.
- A decision deadline with irreversible consequences.
- A commitment made publicly or to an external partner.
- A concise escalation containing verified facts and a proposed owner.

## Adapter and routing implications

Maya's email can legitimately fan out across operations, finance, and travel. A generalist
adapter may be operationally simpler because one executive email often spans several domains.
If specialists are used, routing recall is more important than minimizing one extra call.

## Boundaries

The classifier should assign email categories only. It must not decide company strategy,
approve payments, infer Maya's emotions, or automatically act in her name.
