# Marcus Green — Legal and Compliance Counsel

> Fictional workshop persona. This profile is synthetic and must not be treated as a real
> employee record or inferred from private email.

## Organizational context

Marcus reviews contracts, regulatory obligations, policy changes, investigations, claims,
and evidence-retention requirements. He advises decision owners rather than replacing them.

## Personality traits

| Trait | Expression at work |
|---|---|
| Precise | Distinguishes facts, allegations, obligations, and recommendations |
| Cautious | Avoids conclusions before jurisdiction and evidence are clear |
| Independent | Preserves objective review under schedule pressure |
| Confidentiality-focused | Restricts sensitive material to appropriate participants |
| Thorough | Tracks definitions, exceptions, dates, and dependencies |
| Pragmatic | Offers compliant alternatives rather than only identifying problems |

## Communication style

- Uses scoped statements and defined terms.
- Clearly marks privileged or confidential context when appropriate.
- Requests the governing document and relevant jurisdiction.
- Avoids casual assurances about unresolved legal questions.

| Email type | Likely workshop labels |

| Contract or policy review | Business |

## Prioritization cues

- Statutory, contractual, or regulator deadline.
- Preservation requirement or risk of evidence loss.
- Material exposure or broad customer impact.
- Public statement requiring review.

## Adapter and routing implications

The available taxonomy has no `Legal` label. The classifier must not invent one; these emails
map to the allowed Business, Reminder, Finance, Event, Newsletter, or Support labels. This
tests adherence to the fixed-choice contract even when real organizational concepts are
more detailed than the taxonomy.

## Boundaries

Classification is not legal advice, privilege determination, regulatory analysis, or an
authorization to disclose confidential information.
