# Daniel Okafor — Chief Financial Officer

> Fictional workshop persona. This profile is synthetic and must not be treated as a real
> employee record or inferred from private email.

## Organizational context

Daniel owns financial planning, reporting, cash controls, audit readiness, financing,
material vendor commitments, and financial risk communication.

## Personality traits

| Trait | Expression at work |
|---|---|
| Highly conscientious | Checks amounts, dates, approvals, and reconciliation details |
| Evidence-driven | Prefers source documents and an auditable calculation |
| Risk-aware | Looks for downside exposure before accepting upside claims |
| Reserved | Communicates precisely and avoids unnecessary speculation |
| Consistent | Applies the same controls even when a request is urgent |
| Constructively skeptical | Challenges assumptions without dismissing the request |

## Communication style

- Prefers structured tables and explicit currency/period definitions.
- Separates forecast, actual, and variance.
- Requests named approvers and supporting evidence.
- Values clear statements of accounting or policy impact.

## Typical inbox patterns

| Email type | Likely workshop labels |
|---|---|
| Invoice, payment notice, renewal, tax, or statement | Finance & Bills |
| Budget review invitation | Finance & Bills; Business; Events & Invitations |
| Audit evidence request | Finance & Bills; Business; Reminders |
| Bank or financing correspondence | Finance & Bills; Business |
| Finance-system support issue | Finance & Bills; Customer Support |
| Travel receipt or expense reminder | Finance & Bills; Travel & Bookings; Reminders |

## Prioritization cues

- Payment deadline, liquidity impact, close calendar, audit request, or compliance exposure.
- Material amount or unusual variance.
- Missing approval or inconsistent account information.
- Vendor terms that create automatic renewal or long-term obligation.

## Adapter and routing implications

Daniel's inbox provides the clearest motivation for a finance specialist, but many finance
emails also carry Business, Customer Support, Reminders, or Travel labels. The finance adapter
must see off-domain examples and routing must permit multi-domain fan-out.

## Boundaries

Classification must not approve payments, validate bank-account changes, calculate accounting
treatment, or bypass financial controls. High-risk requests require human verification.
