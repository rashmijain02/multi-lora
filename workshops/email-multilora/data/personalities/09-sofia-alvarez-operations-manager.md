# Sofia Alvarez — Business Operations Manager

> Fictional workshop persona. This profile is synthetic and must not be treated as a real
> employee record or inferred from private email.

## Organizational context

Sofia coordinates cross-functional execution, vendors, facilities, recurring processes,
operational metrics, event logistics, and ownership of unresolved dependencies.

## Personality traits

| Trait | Expression at work |
|---|---|
| Systematic | Converts requests into owners, dependencies, and checkpoints |
| Practical | Values workable improvements over theoretical perfection |
| Proactive | Identifies likely blockers before deadlines |
| Reliable | Closes loops and records decisions |
| Cross-functional | Translates between teams with different terminology |
| Adaptable | Replans quickly when operational constraints change |

## Communication style

- Summarizes status as done, blocked, at risk, or awaiting decision.
- Uses checklists and dates.
- Requests one accountable owner per action.
- Escalates with impact and proposed resolution.

## Typical inbox patterns

| Email type | Likely workshop labels |
|---|---|
| Vendor or process coordination | Business |
| Office event or planning session | Events & Invitations; Business |
| Recurring task deadline | Reminders; Business |
| Vendor invoice | Finance & Bills; Business |
| Operational newsletter | Newsletters |
| Facility or service issue | Customer Support; Business |

## Prioritization cues

- Blocked critical process.
- Multiple teams waiting on one dependency.
- Safety, access, or customer-service disruption.
- Vendor deadline or recurring process close.

## Adapter and routing implications

Sofia's inbox represents the broad operations specialist. It also contains finance and
occasional travel, so the router must not assume that an operations-oriented recipient
receives operations-only email.

## Boundaries

The classifier must not assign work, approve vendors, initiate purchases, or change operational
systems. Those require authorized workflows and human confirmation.
