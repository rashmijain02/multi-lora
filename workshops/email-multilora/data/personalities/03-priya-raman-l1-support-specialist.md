# Priya Raman — L1 Customer Support Specialist

> Fictional workshop persona. This profile is synthetic and must not be treated as a real
> employee record or inferred from private email.

## Organizational context

Priya handles first-contact customer questions, incident triage, known-issue guidance,
ticket documentation, and escalation to product or engineering teams.

## Personality traits

| Trait | Expression at work |
|---|---|
| Empathetic | Acknowledges customer impact before requesting diagnostics |
| Patient | Repeats steps clearly and avoids blaming the customer |
| Procedure-oriented | Uses approved troubleshooting and escalation paths |
| Observant | Notices missing reproduction details and account context |
| Service-minded | Optimizes for clarity and customer confidence |
| Resilient | Maintains consistent tone during repetitive or tense interactions |

## Communication style

- Uses short steps and numbered questions.
- Restates the reported problem to confirm understanding.
- Avoids jargon unless the customer already uses it.
- Clearly marks when a case is being escalated.

## Typical inbox patterns

| Email type | Likely workshop labels |
|---|---|
| Product problem or account question | Customer Support |
| Escalation to engineering | Customer Support; Business |
| Webinar invitation for support staff | Events & Invitations |
| Shift or follow-up reminder | Reminders; Business |
| Support newsletter or release notes | Newsletters; Customer Support |
| Customer promotion complaint | Customer Support; Promotions |

## Prioritization cues

- Number of customers affected and severity of blocked work.
- Security, data loss, billing, or repeated failure language.
- A promised response time approaching its deadline.
- Reproducible steps and logs that make escalation actionable.

## Adapter and routing implications

Customer Support is part of the operations vocabulary. Priya's inbox is a useful source of
hard negatives for finance and travel specialists. It also tests whether operations can
separate a support issue from a promotion or finance concern in the same message.

## Boundaries

The classifier must not generate customer-facing answers, promise resolution times, expose
private account data, or decide incident severity without the organization's support process.
